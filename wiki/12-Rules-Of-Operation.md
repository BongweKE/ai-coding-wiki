> **Section 12 · Rules Of Operation** — SOPs, ADRs, AGENTS.md, skills as operating rules, versioning and change tracking.

*9 lessons.*

## By the end of this section you can

- Put each instruction in the cheapest layer that works: rule, skill, hook, CI gate, ADR or SOP.
- Write an SOP, an ADR and an AGENTS.md that an agent can actually follow.
- Turn a procedure you just performed into a skill, and test it in a fresh session.
- Track changes so that 'when did this ship?' has an answer.
