> **Section 15 · Capstones** — Three capstones, a 30-day plan, and where to keep learning afterwards.

*6 lessons.*

## By the end of this section you can

- Ship a tiny app end to end: API, database, CI, staging, production, README.
- Add an AI feature with a guardrail, an eval set, cost telemetry and a fallback.
- Harden a repository so a stranger — human or agent — is productive in thirty minutes.
- Follow a day-by-day plan for the next thirty days, and keep going after that.

### [Capstone 1: Ship A Tiny App](15-Capstone-1-Ship-A-Tiny-App)
`beginner` · ~45 min — Every lesson so far was a rehearsal; this capstone is the performance — one small product, in public, with a pipeline that deploys it and a README a stranger can follow.

### [Capstone 2: Add An AI Feature Safely](15-Capstone-2-Add-An-AI-Feature)
`intermediate` · ~45 min — Capstone 1 gave you a product with a pipeline.

### [Capstone 3: Make A Repo Agent-Proof](15-Capstone-3-Agent-Proof-Repository)
`intermediate` · ~35 min — A repo where you are productive and one where an agent is productive are not the same repo.

### [The 30-Day Learning Plan](15-30-Day-Learning-Plan)
`beginner` · ~15 min — A 135-lesson wiki is easy to start and easy to abandon in week two.

### [Further Learning](15-Further-Learning)
`beginner` · ~12 min — This wiki is deliberately shallow in one direction: it covers a lot quickly and stops where the field becomes a moving target.

### [Contributing To This Wiki](15-Contributing-To-This-Wiki)
`beginner` · ~8 min — You have just spent the wiki's words. The fastest way to find out whether you learned them is to add one of your own — and this is the safest pull request you will ever open.
